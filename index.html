How the motion runs

Your crew leads the sale. We fund the work, then fade into the background.

A clear division of labour across five stages. Your people own the client relationship start to finish; our role shrinks over time until we're simply the engine behind the answers.

The economics

Paid to build it, paid to keep it.

Two revenue moments for your team: one during enablement, one that recurs for the life of every account. This is a premium capability, priced accordingly — not a discount play.

Paid enablement, up front

Your crew is compensated handsomely for the time they put in making the calls, leading the recorded sessions, and running the intake — before a single deal has to close.

Recurring after install

Once an account is live, there's an ongoing line: your team keeps gathering feedback and serving the client while our models do the heavy lifting behind them.

Premium by design

Engagements carry a firm minimum — this is not a cheap capability and it isn't priced like one. The floor holds because bundling multiple upgrades per account makes each visit worth far more than a single line item.

Exact fee schedule and revenue-share terms are set together in a direct conversation. The principle is simple: your people are paid well for the work, the pricing floor is protected, and the value per account compounds as upgrades stack.

Behind the offer

A proven engine — not a pitch on paper.

What your crew is selling already runs in production. The early mistakes were made and paid for on prior work, so every new account inherits the hardened version. Pointing it at a new client is a configuration, not a rebuild.

  • Live on real data today. A working capability to point at a client's systems — not a pilot to build from scratch.
  • Every answer is replayable. The client's risk and audit teams can verify it themselves — nothing taken on trust.
  • One engine, many uses. The same capability serves finance, billing, staffing, and operations — so upgrades bundle naturally.
  • Built to pass a security review. Scoped, revocable access; nothing leaves the client's environment.
