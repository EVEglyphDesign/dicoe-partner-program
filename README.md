## The model, in short

1. We hand Jeff's crew the **call list**, an **approved message**, an **intake process**, and **recorded sessions** to lead from.
2. His people **run the calls, intake, and demo** — putting a slate of upgrades on the table at once. *We pay them handsomely for their time.*
3. On close, **we install** the capability. His crew stays the face of the account.
4. Post-install, his people **keep gathering client feedback** against outlines we provide. *Recurring revenue line.*
5. We **fade into the background** — DICOE charges to Jeff's team, we supply outlines and take back recordings, and our models help his crew keep serving the account.

**Pricing** is premium by design — a firm minimum per engagement, with value compounding as upgrades bundle. Exact figures are held for a direct conversation.
