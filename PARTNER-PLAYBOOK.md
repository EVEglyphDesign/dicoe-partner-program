# Partner Playbook — Decision Intelligence COE

**Prepared for:** Jeff Eden and team
**Status:** Confidential
**Purpose:** How your crew opens new dealership accounts with a proven AI capability — the roles, the sequence, and the money.

---

## The offer in one line

We hand your crew the call list, the approved message, and recorded sessions to lead the room. Your people run the sale. We fund the enablement work up front, install after the close, and then fade into the background — charging from the Decision Intelligence COE (DICOE) to your team while your people keep the account.

---

## What you receive up front

| Asset | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Qualified call list** | The dealership accounts and the right people to call — the whitespace your crew isn't selling into today. Names, not a cold market. |
| **Approved message** | Pre-vetted outreach and talk track. Consistent, on-brand, already tested — nobody improvises the pitch. |
| **Intake process** | A repeatable process for running the client conversation from first call to signed interest. |
| **Recorded sessions** | A library of recorded intake and demo sessions your people lead from, so every rep runs the same high-quality playbook. |
| **Upgrade slate** | The set of upgrades your crew puts on the table together — more value per account, per visit. |

---

## The five-stage motion

1. **Handover** — We give your crew the list, message, intake process, and recorded sessions. *We pay for your crew's time.*
2. **Your people run intake & demo** — Your reps make the calls, lead the recorded sessions, and put the full upgrade slate on the table at once. They own the room; we're on tap. *Paid enablement phase.*
3. **Sale closes → we install** — When the deal comes through, we handle the technical installation of the capability inside the client's environment. Your crew stays the face of the account. *Transition to recurring.*
4. **Your crew keeps gathering feedback** — After install, your people continue collecting client feedback against simple outlines we provide (what to ask, what to capture). *Ongoing revenue line.*
5. **We fade into the background** — From here we charge from DICOE to your team, hand your people the outlines of what to get from each client, and take back recordings. Our models help your crew keep serving the account. *DICOE → your team.*

## The economics

Two revenue moments for your team:

- **Paid enablement, up front** — your crew is compensated handsomely for the time they put into the calls, the recorded sessions, and the intake — before a deal has to close.
- **Recurring after install** — once an account is live, there's an ongoing line as your team keeps gathering feedback and serving the client while our models do the heavy lifting behind them.

**Premium by design.** Engagements carry a firm minimum. This is not a cheap capability and it isn't priced like one — the floor holds because bundling multiple upgrades per account makes each visit worth far more than a single line item.

*Exact fee schedule and revenue-share terms are set together in a direct conversation. The principle: your people are paid well for the work, the pricing floor is protected, and value per account compounds as upgrades stack.*

## Why it's low-risk to sell

What your crew is selling already runs in production — not a pitch on paper.

- **Live on real data today** — a working capability to point at a client's systems, not a pilot to build from scratch.
- **Every answer is replayable** — the client's risk and audit teams can verify it themselves; nothing taken on trust.
- **One engine, many uses** — the same capability serves finance, billing, staffing, and operations, so upgrades bundle naturally.
- **Built to pass a security review** — scoped, revocable access; nothing leaves the client's environment.

Pointing the engine at a new client is a configuration, not a rebuild. Every new account inherits the hardened version — the early mistakes were made and paid for on prior work.
